Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a carbon footprint and why is it measured in CO₂e?

A carbon footprint measures the total greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions caused directly and indirectly by an individual or organization. Because different gases (like methane and nitrous oxide) trap heat at different intensities, they are converted into Carbon Dioxide Equivalent (CO₂e) using their Global Warming Potential (GWP) over 100 years.

Why does ORCHIDS use India-specific electricity factors?

Electricity emission intensity varies dramatically worldwide based on power generation sources. In India, coal represents a significant portion of base power, resulting in ~0.716 kg CO₂e/kWh according to the Central Electricity Authority (CEA Baseline Database v19). Generic international calculators that use US or European grid averages significantly miscalculate Indian emissions.

Is my data stored securely?

Yes. ORCHIDS operates on a transparent, client-side first architecture. Your calculation records and eco-challenge completions are stored locally in your browser's LocalStorage. No personal information is secretly transmitted or sold to third-party ad networks.

1. Governing Calculation Principles

ORCHIDS calculates greenhouse gas emissions using the IPCC (Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change) activity-based inventory methodology. The mathematical relationship governing every category is:

Emission (kg CO₂e) = Activity Metric (kWh, km, meals, kg) × Validated Emission Factor (kg CO₂e/unit)

For household energy and cooking fuels, shared consumption is distributed across residents using per-capita allocation:

Individual Monthly Energy (kg CO₂e) = Total Household Energy Emissions / Household Size

3. Important Assumptions & Scientific Limitations

  • Model Scope: ORCHIDS covers personal Scope 1 (direct vehicle combustion) and Scope 2 (purchased home electricity) emissions, alongside primary Scope 3 lifecycle activities (food consumption, waste, commercial air travel).
  • Approximations: User inputs rely on user memory and approximations rather than utility-grade submetering. Results are indicative estimates for lifestyle awareness rather than legal GHG regulatory filings.
  • Embodied Emissions: Embodied emissions in durable consumer electronics, heavy furniture, and housing construction are excluded to avoid double-counting and maintain user survey accessibility.
